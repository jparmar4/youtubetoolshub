# Ranking Recovery Checklist — Off-Site Actions (You Execute)

The on-site work (real author identity, honest content, consolidation, noindex triage,
report methodology) removes the quality-suppression signals. But **no on-site change can
replace authority**. An 11-month-old domain with zero backlinks cannot outrank
Influencer Marketing Hub (DR 90+) on "youtube money calculator" no matter how perfect the
page is. Links and brand mentions are the remaining lever — and they only move if you
do these consistently.

Work through this top-to-bottom. Items 1–3 matter most.

---

## 1. Google Search Console (30 minutes, do today)

- [ ] Submit the updated sitemap: GSC → Sitemaps → resubmit `sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Request recrawling (URL Inspection → Request Indexing) for the pages that changed most:
      the homepage, `/about`, `/blog/author/jitu`, the definitive guide
      `/blog/how-much-youtube-pays-per-1000-views-2026`, `/research/youtube-cpm-rpm-report-2026`.
- [ ] After 2–3 weeks, check **Pages → Why pages aren't indexed**. The retired URLs (7 blog
      posts + 41 country pages + 51 state pages) will drop out — that is intended. The
      remaining ~230 URLs should trend toward "Indexed".
- [ ] Watch **Performance** monthly: impressions for "how much does youtube pay",
      "youtube cpm by country", "youtube earnings calculator" are the KPI to grow.

## 2. Backlink foundations (the actual ranking lever)

- [ ] **AlternativeTo** — create a listing for YouTube Tools Hub as an alternative to
      TubeBuddy / VidIQ. These pages rank and pass real links.
- [ ] **Product Hunt** — launch the tool suite. Even a modest launch earns links from
      roundup posts.
- [ ] **Tool directory submissions** — futurepedia, theresanaiforthat, aitools.fyi, and
      similar directories accept free-tool listings.
- [ ] **Reddit, genuinely** (r/NewTubers, r/partneredyoutube, r/YouTube_startups): the
      "youtube money calculator" niche already gets shared there. Answer questions where
      your calculator is genuinely the answer, disclose that you built it, never spam.
      Reddit threads are also a primary corpus for AI search retrieval.
- [ ] **Your own YouTube channel** — demo the tools in 3–5 short videos. This is the
      highest-relevance link + brand signal available to you, and YouTube content ranks
      in YouTube SERPs and feeds AI retrieval.
- [ ] **Creator-economy newsletters / blogs** — pitch the CPM report
      (`/research/youtube-cpm-rpm-report-2026`) as a citable data source. It now has a real
      methodology section — that's what makes it citable.
- [ ] Fix any other dead social profiles; keep @JITU20 active with occasional real posts.

## 3. AI search visibility (ChatGPT / Perplexity / AI Overviews)

AI engines cite what their retrieval layer surfaces: Reddit, YouTube, news, and pages
that other sites already reference. The order of operations:

1. Do item 2 first — without external mentions, AI engines never retrieve you.
2. Keep `llms.txt` / `.well-known/ai.txt` accurate (they now describe one real author — done).
3. Get the CPM report cited by at least 2–3 third-party pages; LLMs follow citations.
4. Brand search volume ("youtube tools hub") is a retrieval trigger — your YouTube
   channel and Reddit presence create it.

## 4. Publishing cadence rules (on-site discipline)

- [ ] Never publish more than 1 post per day. Same-day bursts (7 posts on Feb 4,
      12 on Oct 1) are a scaled-content signature — that pattern is now in the past data,
      so don't recreate it.
- [ ] New posts: one at a time, written or heavily edited by you, with claims you can
      defend. "I tested X" only if you actually tested X and can show it.
- [ ] Only add programmatic pages (country/state/templates) after the core pages rank.

## 5. What NOT to do

- Don't buy backlinks or join link-exchange schemes — on a young site, that's the
  fastest route to a manual action.
- Don't re-expand the noindexed country/state templates until Tier-1 pages rank.
- Don't create new bylines/personas. Every guide is authored by Jitu — that consistency
  is the entity signal.

## 6. Timeline expectations (honest)

- **Weeks 1–4:** consolidation effects — GSC shows retired URLs dropping, impressions
  stabilizing.
- **Months 2–4:** first movement on long-tail queries ("youtube cpm [small country]",
  "[state] creator taxes" style) as suppression lifts and relevance concentrates.
- **Months 4–12:** calculator/CPM head terms only move with real backlinks from item 2.
  Without item 2, expect long-tail wins only.

Track monthly in GSC: total impressions, average position for the 5 priority queries,
and indexed-page count. If impressions are flat after 3 months of item 2, the problem
is link quality — invest there.
